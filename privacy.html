Privacy

Edition of 6 October 2026

Brokra has no sign-in and no Brokra server. Below, the questions people ask about it, answered in order of importance.

Question oneDoes anyone see how I score?

No. Each answer you give, the topic and exam section it belongs to, your finished drills and mock exams, and the list of questions you missed are stored by the app in a local database on your iPhone. They are never uploaded. Without a network, practice still works because the whole question bank is bundled with the download.

Question twoWhat preferences does it remember?

The exam you picked, your optional test date, and your daily study length of 15, 30 or 60 minutes. These sit in the phone's app settings storage and stay there.

Question threeWho handles the subscription?

Apple takes the payment. To work out whether you hold Brokra Pro, the app consults Adapty (adapty.io), a billing-infrastructure provider. Adapty is sent purchase receipts, an anonymous profile identifier, the app version and iOS version, your storefront country, and step counters from the first-run flow. Our App Store privacy label lists this as device identifier and purchase history used for app functionality, plus product interaction for analytics, kept apart from your identity and never used to track you.

Your quiz results are not part of what Adapty receives.

Question fourDoes Brokra track me, show ads or read my contacts?

None of those. There are no ad networks, no cross-app tracking, and the app never requests your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or health data. It does not send notifications.

Question fiveDoes it ask for an App Store rating?

Occasionally, after you finish a mock exam and only from your third session, at most about once every ninety days. The prompt is Apple's own system dialog, so Apple, not us, receives whatever you choose to write.

Question sixHow do I wipe it?

  1. Delete Brokra from your iPhone. That clears answers, mocks, review queue and settings stored on the device. The app has no separate reset button.
  2. To remove the Adapty-side profile too, email the address in the footer and we will send the deletion request.

Question sevenAny age limits?

The material is for adults preparing for professional registration. We do not direct Brokra to children under 13.

Question eightWhat if this changes?

The edition date at the head of the page will move. Write to nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru with anything else.
