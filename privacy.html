Privacy Policy

Brokra: Series 7 Exam Prep · Last updated September 28, 2026

Brokra is an offline study app for the FINRA Series 7 and SIE and the NASAA Series 63, 65 and 66 exams. There are no accounts and no server that stores your answers. We don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your answers, session and mock scores, and your mistakes queue.
  • What you told us in onboarding: which exam, whether you passed the SIE, your test window, who sponsors the exam and your daily study time.

This is stored only in Brokra's own storage on your iPhone and is never uploaded to us.

Purchases and analytics

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Adapty (adapty.io) to show plans and to know whether your membership is active. Adapty receives an anonymous app profile, purchase events and a few onboarding answers (exam, test window, sponsor, onboarding step) to measure which screens work. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location or advertising identifier, and we do not track you across other apps.

Your choices

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored. Questions or requests: nastya.shchaveleva@internet.ru.

Children

Brokra is meant for adults preparing for securities licensing exams and is not directed at children.
